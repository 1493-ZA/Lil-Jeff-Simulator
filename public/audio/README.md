These are original synthesized placeholder voices, generated from the original
lines in lines.json with the stock Flite RMS, KAL16 and AWB voices. They do not
sample or imitate any artist or real person's voice. Flite and voice notices
are included in FLITE-NOTICES.txt. The instrumental is synthesized by src/music.js.

Regenerate: python3 scripts/generate-voices.py (requires system Flite libraries,
FFmpeg and ffprobe). Runtime playback uses the bundled Ogg files; no speech API,
account or remote service is required.

Original percussion samples in drums/ are generated by scripts/generate-drums.py
with Python and NumPy; all six WAV files are bundled for runtime playback. No
external drum libraries or artist recordings are used. The mixer retains a
procedural fallback while these local samples load.
